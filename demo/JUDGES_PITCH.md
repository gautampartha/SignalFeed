# SignalFeed — judges’ pitch

Approximately 500 words. Stage directions are in italics; do not read them aloud.

Good morning, judges.

Imagine opening WhatsApp to hundreds of unread messages. Somewhere in your college community, someone has shared a paid internship that fits your skills perfectly. By the time you find it, applications have closed.

For students, an announcement buried in a conversation can mean a missed first step into their career.

We built **SignalFeed**, a proposed add-on feature for WhatsApp that helps people discover and act on relevant opportunities inside the communities they choose.

Our promise is simple: **never miss an opportunity hidden in a conversation.**

*Open the internship demo and show the Design Circle message.*

Here is our example: a Product Design Internship at Forma. It offers ₹30,000 per month, remote work, and a three-month learning opportunity. Its eligibility, stipend, and deadline are scattered across an announcement poster and a message caption.

The same opportunity is shared across several communities, mixed in with discussions, greetings, and unrelated messages.

SignalFeed brings the useful details together in one card: the role, company, stipend, location, eligibility, and application deadline. It also keeps a link to the original message so the user can inspect the source.

*Show the opportunity card, then save it and set a reminder.*

Finding an opportunity is only the beginning. I can save it to my shortlist and set a reminder a day before applications close. I can also export that reminder to my calendar.

That gives me a clear next step: prepare my portfolio and apply on time.

Personalization is central to this feature. The user chooses both **what they care about** and **which communities contribute to their feed**. Communities start excluded. Users can add interests, select sources individually, and remove them whenever they want.

For example, if I choose Sports and Campus Sports Club, I see relevant football, badminton, and sports-related announcements. If I choose Internships and my career community, I see relevant roles. Users can also create their own local demo communities and interests.

*Briefly show community selection or the Sports feed.*

Our prototype includes six communities, eleven distinct group conversations, and fifteen sample opportunities, including ten paid internships. It runs as a responsive web app and inside an iOS WebView, with persistent preferences, saved opportunities, reminders, and dark mode.

For this hackathon, messages and employers are fictional, and AI extraction is simulated. The filtering, source selection, conversations, saving, reminder management, and calendar export are interactive. A production version would require permitted message access, live extraction, and a notification scheduler.

We would measure success through relevant opportunities discovered, applications started, and deadlines met. Those outcomes connect the feature directly to the student’s goal.

WhatsApp communities already bring people and opportunities together. SignalFeed helps users turn those conversations into action.

**Your next opportunity may already be in your chats. SignalFeed helps you find it—and take the next step.**

Thank you.
